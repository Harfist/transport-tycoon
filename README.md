# Burrow & Blade: Rabbit Colony Simulation

A 2D top-down fantasy medieval colony simulation combining the citizen depth and family reproduction of *Cultures* with the colony management and world map exploration of *RimWorld*.

Live Demo: [harfist.github.io/transport-tycoon](https://harfist.github.io/transport-tycoon/)

## Core Mechanics & Design

### 1. The Rabbit Race & Cultures Life Cycle
- **Every Citizen Matters:** Every rabbit has an individual name, gender (Buck or Doe), age, home burrow, spouse, hunger, mood, and profession.
- **Housing & Marriage:** Rabbits court and marry, moving into cozy thatched earthen Burrows together as family units.
- **Reproduction & Kits:** When a married couple lives in a burrow and enjoys abundant food (Carrots and baked Carrot Bread) and happiness, they bear newborn Kits!
- **Coming of Age & Apprenticeships:** Kits hop and play around the village green and hearth. Upon turning 5, they come of age into adults and can be assigned to specialized professions:
  - 🥕 **Carrot Farmer:** Cultivates tilled carrot patches.
  - 🌾 **Wheat Farmer:** Tends grain fields.
  - 🥖 **Baker:** Mills flour and bakes hearty Carrot Bread.
  - 🪓 **Woodcutter:** Fells timber from forest groves.
  - ⛏️ **Stone Mason:** Hews quarry boulders into construction stone.
  - 🔨 **Artisan Craftsman:** Crafts tools, furniture, and trade wagons.
  - 🎒 **Caravan Trader:** Travels across the regional world map.
  - 👴 **Elders:** Senior rabbits (age 45+) retire to guide the community.

### 2. Two-Tier View: Local Warren & Regional World Map
- **Settlement View (Micro):** Top-down 2D bird's-eye view with smooth zoom and pan, cobblestone speed paths, burrows, agricultural zones, workshops, and animated hopping rabbit pawns.
- **World Map (Macro):** Regional grid showing your rabbit warren (*Brambleton*) surrounded by neighboring animal kingdoms:
  - 🦡 **Krag-Hollow (Badgers):** Mountain blacksmiths and miners (trades Iron and Gold).
  - 🦦 **Otter-Weir (Otters):** River fishers and pearl merchants (trades Salt and Pearls).
  - 🦊 **Reynar's Hold (Foxes):** Frontier weavers and traders (trades Fine Silk and Gold).
  - 🦔 **Quill-Grove (Hedgehogs):** Forest herbalists and woodcarvers (trades Herbs and Gold).

### 3. Trade Caravans & Economy
- Form trade caravans at the colony, pack them with bread, carrots, and craft goods, and dispatch them across the world map.
- Caravans travel in real-time between territories to complete lucrative trades, returning home with gold sovereigns to enrich the colony treasury.
